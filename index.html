Operating model

From real estate to hospitality.

One operator can support the asset at any stage. Early involvement is valuable, but not required.

Market, demand, competition, property potential and commercial feasibility.

Reservations, guest support, revenue management, housekeeping, maintenance and quality control.

ADR, occupancy, RevPAR, channel mix, direct bookings, reviews, costs and owner returns.

Privacy notice.

When you submit a stay request or property opportunity, Sfera uses the details you provide only to review, respond to and manage that request.

Submissions are delivered to Sfera’s operational email and Respond.io workspace. Optional property documents are delivered to Sfera’s operational email for review. Access is limited to the team handling guest requests and property opportunities. Sfera does not sell submitted personal information.

Google Analytics is optional and loads only after you select “Allow analytics.” It collects usage information such as page views and interactions to help Sfera improve the website. You can change your analytics preference at any time.

To request access, correction or deletion of submitted information, email prishtinacityapartments@gmail.com.

About PRI

Comfort, privacy and room to settle in.

PRI Aparthotel offers recently renovated accommodation with air conditioning, private bathrooms and free Wi‑Fi. Depending on the room type, stays may also include a kitchenette, balcony, dining space and city views.

The property is suited to short city stays as well as guests who want a little more independence, with free private parking on site and private check-in/check-out.
